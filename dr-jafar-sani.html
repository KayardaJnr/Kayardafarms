Chief Veterinary Officer

Dr. Jafar Sani

Dr. Jafar Sani is a distinguished veterinarian with specialized expertise in poultry and aquaculture health. As Chief Veterinary Officer at Kayarda Farms, he leads our veterinary services, biosecurity programs, and research initiatives in animal nutrition and health management.

With a Doctor of Veterinary Medicine (DVM) degree and advanced training in poultry science, Dr. Sani oversees all aspects of animal health across our integrated operations. His work encompasses disease prevention, vaccination programs, biosecurity protocols, and the development of specialized feed formulations that optimize animal health and productivity.

Dr. Sani has published research on poultry health management and aquaculture nutrition. He is actively involved in industry associations and regularly conducts training programs for farmers on best practices in animal health and biosecurity. His expertise ensures that Kayarda maintains the highest standards in animal welfare and food safety.

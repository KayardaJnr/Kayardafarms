Chief Executive Officer

Alhaji Sani Umar

Visionary leader and founder with over 20 years of experience in integrated agribusiness

Alhaji Sani Umar brings over 20 years of experience in integrated agribusiness to Kayarda Farms. As the visionary leader and founder, he has been instrumental in transforming Kayarda from a small local operation into one of Nigeria's leading integrated poultry and aquaculture enterprises.

His strategic vision encompasses the entire value chain - from feed production and hatchery operations to live bird marketing and processing. Under his leadership, Kayarda has established state-of-the-art facilities across multiple locations, implementing best practices in biosecurity, quality assurance, and sustainable farming.

Alhaji Umar holds a degree in Agricultural Economics and has received numerous industry recognitions for his contributions to Nigerian agriculture. He is passionate about developing sustainable food systems that benefit both farmers and consumers across West Africa.

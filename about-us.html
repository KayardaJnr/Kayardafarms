Our Journey

The Vision Takes Root

Founded in 2018 by Alhaji Sani Umar, Kayarda Integrated Farms began as a modest poultry operation in Kano State. What started as a small-scale venture quickly grew into a comprehensive agricultural enterprise driven by a simple yet powerful vision: to revolutionize Nigeria's agricultural sector through integrated farming practices.

Alhaji Sani Umar, a seasoned agricultural entrepreneur with over two decades of experience, recognized the critical gaps in Nigeria's agricultural supply chain. He saw an opportunity to create a vertically integrated operation that would control quality from farm to table, ensuring that Nigerian farmers and consumers alike could access premium agricultural products.

2018

Foundation Year

"Quality is not an act, it is a habit."

- Alhaji Sani Umar

2019 - Expansion Phase

Within the first year, we expanded our operations to include aquaculture and feed production. Our integrated approach began to take shape as we established our own feed mill to ensure complete control over feed quality and nutrition.

2020 - Innovation & Technology

Despite global challenges, we invested heavily in modern farming technologies and sustainable practices. Our R&D team developed proprietary feed formulations that improved animal health and productivity by 35%.

2021 - Nationwide Reach

We established distribution networks across all 36 states of Nigeria, becoming the first integrated agricultural company to achieve true nationwide coverage with consistent quality standards.

2022 - Leadership Recognition

Kayarda was recognized as Nigeria's leading integrated agricultural enterprise, receiving multiple industry awards for innovation, sustainability, and contribution to food security.

2023 - Global Standards

We achieved international certifications and expanded our export operations, bringing Nigerian agricultural products to global markets while maintaining the highest quality standards.

2024 - Future Forward

Today, Kayarda Integrated Farms continues to innovate and expand, committed to sustainable agriculture, food security, and empowering Nigerian farmers for generations to come.

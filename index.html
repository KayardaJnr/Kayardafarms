Who We Are

Welcome to Kayarda Farms

Kayarda Integrated Farms is a diversified agricultural enterprise committed to excellence in food production and supply. We operate across poultry farming, aquaculture, crop supply, fertilizer distribution, and logistics services—delivering quality products and reliable solutions to meet the growing demands of the agricultural sector.

Through continuous growth, innovation, and strategic expansion into poultry, aquaculture, crop supply, and logistics, we have built a brand that reflects precision, consistency, and trust. Our journey is guided by a commitment to delivering superior products while contributing meaningfully to agricultural advancement.
